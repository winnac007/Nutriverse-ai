# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People reading a personalized nutrition and lifestyle blueprint on a phone. They need calm, legible guidance that can be understood quickly without zooming or decoding dense layouts.

## Product Purpose

ZenPlato turns personal health information into a premium, practical wellness guide. The ebook should help readers understand patterns, connect nutrition and lifestyle choices, and take sustainable daily action.

## Brand Personality

Calm, reassuring, and editorial. The experience should feel like a premium wellness journal: warm, considered, personal, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Cramped poster layouts, tiny body text, overlapping copy, cropped focal imagery, excessive empty space, generic dashboard styling, and visual changes that break continuity with the existing ebook.

## Design Principles

- Readability is part of the premium experience, especially on a phone.
- Preserve the established botanical, photographic, and editorial visual language.
- Give each page one clear reading sequence and enough room for dynamic content.
- Personalization should feel integrated, never pasted over the artwork.
- Favor calm pacing and useful guidance over maximum information density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body copy must remain readable without zooming, retain clear contrast against imagery, and avoid collisions at supported phone sizes. Navigation and page labels should remain keyboard and screen-reader accessible.
